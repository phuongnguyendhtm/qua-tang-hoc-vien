# FreeUp — Foundation Builder (Steps 1-6)
## Complete Foundation Setup · All-in-One Skill

---

## What You'll Build (6 Steps)

This skill guides you through the **foundation** of a successful business — everything before you write copy or launch ads.

1. **Avatar Builder** — Who is your ideal customer? What do they want, fear, believe?
2. **Brand Voice** — How should AI write in your voice/style?
3. **Hero Mechanism** — What makes you different? Your proprietary method?
4. **Money Model** — How will you make money? What offers at what price?
5. **Offer Architect** — How do you package each offer to make it irresistible?
6. **HVCO Creator** — What free lead magnet attracts the right people?

By the end, you'll have:
- ✓ Detailed customer avatar
- ✓ Voice profile (for all future AI writing)
- ✓ Hero mechanism (your differentiation)
- ✓ Money model (4+ revenue streams)
- ✓ 3+ offers architected
- ✓ Lead magnet brief ready to write

## How to Use This Skill

**Preparation:** Have ready:
- What you sell (or planning to sell)
- Who you think your customers are
- Basic business info

**Process:** 
- Answer questions for each step
- Read outputs — they become input for next step
- Keep all output files (you'll need them later for writing copy)

**Time:** 2-3 hours total (can split across days)

---

## Step 1: Avatar Builder

> **Purpose:** Build a complete Dream Buyer Avatar — understand your ideal customer deeply.

**You'll answer:**
- Who are they? (demographics)
- What do they want? (desires)
- What do they fear? (pain points)
- What objections will they have?
- Where do they hang out? (where to advertise)

**Output:** `avatar-[name].md` file

---

## Step 2: Brand Voice Builder

> **Purpose:** Create a Voice Profile so AI writes in YOUR voice, not corporate-speak.

**You'll provide:**
- 2-3 samples of your writing
- Your tone preferences
- Your communication style

**Output:** `voice-profile-[name].md` file

**Why it matters:** Same offer written in different voices attracts different people. Make sure yours is authentic.

---

## Step 3: Hero Mechanism Builder

> **Purpose:** Build your proprietary mechanism — the thing that makes you incomparable.

**You'll define:**
- Your core method/process
- Why it's different
- The framework or system name
- How it solves the problem uniquely

**Output:** `hero-mechanism-[name].md` file

**Why it matters:** Without differentiation, customers compare you on price. With a hero mechanism, you're in a category of one.

---

## Step 4: Money Model Architect

> **Purpose:** Map complete revenue using Hormozi's 4-offer model (Attraction + Upsell + Downsell + Continuity).

**You'll map:**
- What's your Attraction offer? (entry point)
- What's your Upsell? (premium product)
- What's your Downsell? (lower price option)
- What's your Continuity? (recurring revenue)

**Output:** `money-model-[name].md` file

**Why it matters:** Most businesses leave money on the table. A proper money model 3-5x's revenue from same customers.

---

## Step 5: Offer Architect

> **Purpose:** Build irresistible offers using Sabri Suby + Hormozi's packaging formulas.

**You'll design:**
- Core outcome (what they get)
- Mechanism (how they get it)
- Packaging (course vs. done-for-you vs. community)
- Price (using proven formulas)
- Guarantee (risk reversal)

**Output:** `offer-[name]-[offer-type].md` files (one per offer)

**Why it matters:** Bad offer kills everything. Even great copy can't sell a weak offer.

---

## Step 6: HVCO Creator

> **Purpose:** Design a High-Value Content Offer (free lead magnet) that attracts ideal customers.

**You'll brainstorm:**
- What problem should it solve?
- What format? (checklist, guide, template, quiz, challenge?)
- How valuable? (people should *want* to share it)
- What's the copy brief for writing it?

**Output:** `hvco-[name]-brief.md` file

**Why it matters:** Lead magnet determines your entire funnel quality. Cheap lead magnet = cheap customers.
